About BuyBIBZ

Cambodia's trusted online marketplace — quality products, fair prices, fast delivery nationwide.

Our Story

BuyBIBZ was founded in 2024 in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, with a clear mission: to bring convenient, affordable, and trustworthy online shopping to Cambodian consumers. We saw that many shoppers struggled with unreliable sellers, overpriced products, and slow deliveries — so we built something better.

Today, BuyBIBZ serves customers across all 25 provinces of Cambodia, offering thousands of carefully curated products — from electronics and fashion to home essentials and beauty. We work directly with trusted suppliers and brands to guarantee authentic products at competitive prices, with delivery in 10–13 days.

Our Mission

To empower Cambodian shoppers with a modern, reliable, and user-friendly e-commerce platform that offers genuine products at fair prices — making quality shopping accessible to everyone, everywhere in Cambodia.

Our Vision

To become Cambodia's leading e-commerce platform, trusted by millions of customers for quality, convenience, and value — while supporting local businesses and contributing to the growth of the digital economy in Southeast Asia.

100% Authentic

Every product is sourced directly from verified suppliers and brands. We guarantee authenticity and quality on every order.

Nationwide Delivery

Fast delivery to all 25 provinces. Phnom Penh orders arrive in 10–12 days, provinces in 12–13 days.

24/7 Support

Our dedicated customer service team is available via phone, email, and Telegram to help you with any questions or issues.
